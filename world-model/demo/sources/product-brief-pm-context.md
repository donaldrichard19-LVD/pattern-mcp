# Product brief: PM context for written artifacts

Audience: product leadership. Owner: Priya Nair.

> Demo document, fictional.

## Why now
Documents are where product direction lives, but they fragment across tools. Designers and engineers told us they cannot tell which document is the current source of truth.

## Who we serve
Product managers who write PRDs and briefs. Cross-functional teammates in design and engineering who read them to decide what to build.

## What they are trying to do
- PMs want to write a defensible PRD without starting from scratch.
- Teammates want to understand why a feature exists without asking the PM.

## What gets in the way
- Context is scattered across documents, Slack threads and meeting notes.
- Documents go stale after decisions are made elsewhere.

## Open questions
- Will PMs trust AI-suggested metrics enough to keep them in the document?
- Are AI meeting notes a primary input to PRDs, or only a reference?
