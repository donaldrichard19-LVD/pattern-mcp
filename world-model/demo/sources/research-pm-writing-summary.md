# Research summary: how PMs write and maintain PRDs and briefs

> Demo document, fictional. Eight 45-minute interviews with product managers, plus four with designers and engineers.

## Findings
1. Seven of eight PMs said they start a PRD by copying the last one they wrote.
2. Six of eight PMs said the success metrics section is the part they rewrite or skip.
3. Most PMs said they edit AI-suggested text heavily before sharing it with anyone.
4. Engineers and designers said they usually read the one-page brief and only open the PRD to check details.
5. Five PMs said documents are out of date within two weeks of kickoff because decisions are made in Slack and never written back.
6. Four PMs described pasting AI meeting notes into a draft and spending twenty minutes or more reshaping them into problem, outcome and metric headings.

## Implications
Start-from-scratch friction and stale documents are the two most cited pains. Metrics are the weakest section of most drafts.
